Designing for a Neighbourhood

Since 2018 I have volunteered as Lead Designer for the Canyon Meadows Community Association. The job: give a volunteer-run organization one clear, friendly visual identity, and make it flexible enough to carry everything the community does.

That identity now runs through the association’s program logos, website, event posters and handouts, door hangers, membership drives, on-site signage and yearly social campaigns. Each event gets its own personality, from Winterfest to Stampede in September, while still reading as unmistakably CMCA.

This case study gathers eight years of that work. It shows how a consistent system lets a small team look polished, promote events quickly and build real recognition in the neighbourhood it serves.
