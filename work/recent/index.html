Independent Work, Then and Now

Alongside my in-house roles, I have run a freelance practice since 2007, partnering directly with founders and small teams to build identities, websites and print collateral from the ground up.

The projects here range from Benevity’s early corporate identity and website to brand systems for Claraluna Massage & Wellness, Axiom, Kilo-9 Pet Resort and Calvert Home Mortgage. Several of these brands are still in use today, years after launch.

Each engagement starts the same way: understand the business, define what makes it different, then give it a clear visual voice across every touchpoint it needs.
