16 Years of Digital & Brand Evolution

Over a 16-year tenure leading design and digital strategy, I steered the visual identity and online ecosystem for Homes by Avi through a period of massive industry change. My role: bridge high-level business strategy with real visual execution. Across my time there, I focused on building practical, scalable design systems—modernizing how the brand connected with homebuyers across web, print, and regional marketing campaigns.

That work directly moved the needle for our sales and marketing teams. By completely redesigning our core website architecture and simplifying how buyers search for communities and home models, we saw a clear bump in showhome visits, digital brochure downloads, and online leads. At the same time, centralizing our brand assets and UI components cut graphic production timelines dramatically, allowing our team to push campaign assets to market days faster than before.

Homes by Avi was named Builder of the Year multiple times during my tenure, and National Builder of the Year in my final year there.

This case study highlights the evolution of that ecosystem. It shows how bridging solid user experience design with a clean, approachable corporate identity built a brand presence that was easy to use and hard to ignore in a competitive homebuilding market.
