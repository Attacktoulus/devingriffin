Privacy

Last updated November 2026

This is a personal portfolio site. It doesn’t sell anything, run ads or track you across the web. Here is everything it does with your information.

What the contact form collects

When you send a message, the form collects your name, your email address, the subject and your message. That’s all. I use it only to read and reply to your message.

Who handles it

Messages are delivered by Formspree, which stores the submission and forwards it to my inbox. Formspree also filters spam. Your message isn’t shared with anyone else, and it’s never sold or used for marketing lists.

Cookies and analytics

This site doesn’t set cookies or use analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of delivering them.

How long it’s kept

I keep messages only as long as the conversation needs. You can ask me to delete anything you’ve sent at any time.

Questions or requests

Email devingriffin75@gmail.com to ask what I hold about you, correct it or have it deleted.
